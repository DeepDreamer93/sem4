About this study
This MBA project examines how the adoption of Artificial Intelligence in the Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) functions of UK and EU banks, in regulatory monitoring, screening, risk scoring, control testing and compliance reporting, is changing the work of the people in those functions.
Objective: to examine how AI-driven transformation in GRC functions is reshaping workforce management decisions on role structure, skill requirements, staffing and employee adaptation, and to develop a practical framework that banking management can use to plan GRC workforce transformation while sustaining compliance effectiveness and employee engagement.
