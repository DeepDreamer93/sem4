KBC Group

We are an integrated bank-insurance group, catering mainly for retail,
private banking, SME and mid-cap clients. Our core markets are Belgium,
the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary and Bulgaria. We are present to a
limited extent in several other countries.

to our annual report, you will find more detailed information in separate reports at www.kbc.com.
